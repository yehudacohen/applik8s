---
title: GuestBook, Chirp, and Agentic Start
description: Learn from three maintained applications with increasingly demanding semantics.
---

- **GuestBook** is the ten-minute path: one CRD-backed model, reconciliation, reactive query, UI, deployment,
  and source-owned browser journey.
- **Chirp** is the distributed pressure test: transactional models, streams, projections, media, analytics,
  automation, moderation, portable Kubernetes/AWS profiles, and destructive lifecycle evidence.
- **Agentic Start** is the generated product foundation: identity, workspaces, documents, conversations,
  knowledge, reviews, agents, evaluations, billing, operations, and Builder.

Each example is executable against its documented version and links evidence rather than claiming provider
maturity from prose.
